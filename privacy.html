Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

This is a personal portfolio website run by Nafiz Khan from Bangladesh. Visitors can browse without signing in.

What I collect

I do not run analytics or advertising trackers on these pages.

When you contact me

If you use the contact form, Formspree processes the details you submit and forwards the inquiry to me.

The email links on this site open your own email app. If you write to me, I receive your email address and whatever you put in the message. I use it only to reply to you, and I do not share it or sell it.

The feedback form

The feedback link opens a Google Form. Google handles submissions under its privacy policy. If you agree to public display, the display name and message you provide can appear on the portfolio. Leave out private or sensitive information.

Owner dashboard

The owner dashboard uses a session cookie to keep the site owner signed in. The public portfolio does not require an account.

Other websites

Links to GitHub, Instagram and YouTube lead to sites with their own privacy policies.
