Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This is a personal portfolio website run by Nafiz Khan from Bangladesh. It is a static site: there are no accounts, no sign-ups and no online payments.

What I collect

I do not run analytics or advertising trackers on these pages. The site may keep small display preferences in your browser (local storage). They stay on your device and are never sent to me.

When you contact me

The email links on this site open your own email app. If you write to me, I receive your email address and whatever you put in the message. I use it only to reply to you, and I do not share it or sell it.

The feedback form

The feedback form is a Google Form embedded on the home page. What you submit there is received by me and processed by Google under Google's privacy policy. Please do not put anything sensitive in it.

Other websites

Links to GitHub and Instagram lead to sites with their own privacy policies.
