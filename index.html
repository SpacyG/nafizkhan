The process

How we work together

Four steps from your first message to the finished copy.

  1. Send a brief

    I'll read through your project and ask about anything that could affect the work.

    Your next moveSend your project details using the checklist below.

  2. Agree on the quote

    I'll send a fixed quote that sets out the scope, dates, and what's included.

    Your next moveReview the scope, dates, and terms, then approve the quote.

  3. Draft the copy

    I'll review your materials, do the agreed research, and send the draft on the date we agreed.

    Your next moveShare source material and flag any facts or wording I need to keep.

  4. Get the final copy

    I'll revise the draft based on your feedback and send a clean final version.

    Your next moveSend one set of comments per round, then confirm when the copy is ready.

Ready to send a brief?

  • Who it's for

Frequently asked questions

What do you need to get started?

Tell me what you sell, who it’s for, and what you want the reader to do. Existing copy or a link helps.

How long does a project take?

Timing depends on scope. I’ll confirm a first-draft date once we’ve agreed on the brief.

How do you price projects?

I’ll share a quote after we agree on the deliverables and timeline.

Can you revise copy I already have?

Yes. Send the current draft and tell me what you want it to do better.

What’s included in the free headline teardown?

Send one headline and a little context. I’ll point out what’s clear, what’s missing, and one direction to try.
