What I write

Email copywriting
Welcome emails, product launches, and sequences that give readers a reason to come back.

Welcome email “Explore our collection and find your next favourite.”

Sales copy
Sales pages and product descriptions that explain the offer and answer the questions buyers are likely to ask.

Planner sales page “Set your three daily priorities, organize your tasks, and take a moment to reflect at the end of the day.”

Landing pages

03 Approach

How I work

  1. Read. Find out who’s reading and what they need.
  2. Draft. Find the details that make the offer matter and write around one clear message.
  3. Edit. Keep revising until the next step feels obvious.
